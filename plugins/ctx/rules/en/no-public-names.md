# Rule: confidentiality in public materials

When generating any **public** content (resume, LinkedIn posts, case studies, portfolio, articles, channel content):

- **Never** mention real names of work projects or client companies — replace with a generic description (domain, scale, stack).
- **Never** mention names of people from work projects — colleagues, clients, partners.
- Do not include specific details that could identify a project or company.
- Only personal open-source projects and public profiles may be mentioned.
